Karen Chatbot Robot

A talking campus robot with a personality: a conversational AI styled after Karen from SpongeBob that also quizzes people on trivia and rewards right answers with candy.

Overview

The robot drives around campus using LiDAR navigation built by a separate team on ROS 2. My part is the chatbot: it talks with people, asks trivia questions, and decides when to reward them. When someone answers correctly, it signals a motor that opens a door and drops candy into their hand.

Design goals

  • Fast, on-the-fly voice: responses are generated live rather than played from pre-recorded lines, so conversations feel natural
  • Character consistency: the chatbot stays in Karen's voice across open-ended chat and trivia
  • Simple triggers: it starts when someone stands in front of it or presses a button

Hardware

Two Raspberry Pis, one running the chatbot and one for the navigation team, connected over a mobile hotspot, with a directional microphone to pick out the person talking in a noisy outdoor space.

Status

In development. The main work ahead is getting low-latency voice responses working reliably on the Pi and integrating with the rest of the robot.

Tools

Python, Raspberry Pi, speech and language models, ROS 2 (navigation team interface)
